AI & Cognitive Engagement

An interactive research poster exploring cognitive offloading, AI dependency, and educational technology integration.

Scroll down to explore the project. Double click the poster to zoom in.

Main Findings

Students frequently used AI tools for:

  • Information searching
  • Summarization
  • Assignment creation
  • Lesson planning

Teachers highlighted how Chromebook integration normalized AI-assisted learning practices.

Cognitive Offloading

Participants externalized thinking-heavy processes onto AI systems, potentially reducing sustained cognitive engagement.
