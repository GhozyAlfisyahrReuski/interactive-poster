Brief Background

Chromebooks increased accessibility to AI tools in educational settings. Participants highlighted how convenience may unintentionally foster dependency on AI systems.

This section explores how school-issued technology shapes learning behaviors.

Read Source →

Main Findings

Students frequently use AI for:

  • Information searching
  • Summarizing
  • Assignment creation
  • Lesson preparation

Teachers reported increased AI accessibility through Chromebooks.

Cognitive Offloading

Participants often relied on AI to complete thinking-heavy tasks, reducing opportunities for sustained cognitive engagement.

This reflects the concept of cognitive offloading — externalizing mental processes onto technology.
