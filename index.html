Main Findings

Students frequently used AI tools for:

  • Information searching
  • Summarization
  • Assignment creation
  • Lesson planning

Teachers highlighted how Chromebook integration normalized AI-assisted learning practices.

Cognitive Offloading

Participants externalized thinking-heavy processes onto AI systems, potentially reducing sustained cognitive engagement.

Research Highlights

Cognitive Offloading

AI externalizes cognitive tasks, reducing sustained mental engagement.

AI Accessibility

School-issued Chromebooks normalized daily AI usage among students.

Critical Thinking

Teachers emphasized supervised AI use to maintain analytical skills.

Future Concerns

Overreliance on AI may contribute to dependency in academic settings.
